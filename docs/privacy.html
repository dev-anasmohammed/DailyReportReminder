Your data, explained

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers DailyReport, also called Daily Report Reminder, a Windows and macOS application maintained by dev-anasmohammed, and this project website.

In brief: your drafts and settings are stored locally. Optional Gmail access is used to find your report conversation and send a reply you approve. DailyReport does not operate a developer-hosted server for collecting your reports or Google account data.

1. Local report and repository data

When you add a project folder, the app uses your installed Git command-line tool to read commit metadata from local branches. This includes commit subjects, identifiers, author email addresses, and timestamps, so it can select your commits for the current local date. It does not fetch remote repositories as part of report collection or upload your repository files.

The app stores your selected repository paths, configured author email, reminder settings, working hours, report drafts and history, completion state, and Gmail delivery settings and confirmations on your computer. Report drafts and ordinary settings are not encrypted by the app. The text you enter may contain work-related or personal information, so avoid including secrets or information you are not authorized to share.

2. Google account access and use

Gmail integration is optional. If you connect an account, Google asks for your consent through your browser. DailyReport requests these permissions:

  • gmail.metadata: to obtain your Gmail account identity and message metadata needed to select and reply to a conversation. This includes subjects, senders, To/CC/Reply-To addresses, message dates, labels, message and thread identifiers, and threading headers such as Message-ID and References.
  • gmail.send: to send the report from your connected Gmail account after you review the preview and click Send reply.

The app requests message metadata, not full email bodies or attachments. Conversation results are held in memory for selection and reply preparation. Your selected account address, conversation identifier, reply preference, and delivery confirmation details are stored locally. The app does not request permission to delete messages or modify mailbox labels.

OAuth access and refresh tokens and the imported OAuth client configuration are stored using platform secure storage. The app uses refresh tokens to renew access when you next use the Gmail feature. Tokens are not included in report emails, copied reports, or the source repository.

Google API requests go directly from your computer to Google. The report content, recipients, subject, and reply headers are sent to Google when you choose to send. The app provides both HTML and plain-text versions of the report.

Limited Use statement

DailyReport’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the applicable Google Workspace user data and developer policy.

Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing account connection, conversation selection, recipient preparation, and reviewed report-reply features. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to determine creditworthiness, or used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models. The developer does not receive or routinely access your Gmail data through the app.

3. Sharing and third-party services

Sending a report intentionally shares its contents with Google and the recipients shown in your preview. Review those recipients and your organization’s rules before sending. Google and the recipients handle the resulting email under their own policies.

Copy report places report content on your operating system clipboard in formatted and plain-text formats. Other applications you paste into, and applications with clipboard access, may access that content. DailyReport does not control those applications.

DailyReport does not include app analytics, advertising services, or an app-owned cloud report store. If you submit a support request on GitHub, the information you choose to include is processed by GitHub and may be public. Do not include credentials, tokens, private emails, or confidential report text in public issues.

4. Retention, control, and deletion

  • You can edit or clear report text and remove project folders in the app. Removing a project does not automatically erase its previous report text from history.
  • Drafts, report history, account/thread preferences, and send confirmations remain in local application storage until changed or removed. Conversation metadata loaded for the picker is not maintained in a developer-hosted database.
  • Use Settings → Configure Gmail → Disconnect to remove local account access and refresh tokens. The imported desktop OAuth client configuration remains available for reconnection until replaced or removed from the app’s secure-storage data.
  • You can revoke the app’s Google access separately through Google Account connections. Revocation does not delete messages already sent or your local report history.
  • To remove all local app data, close the app and remove its application-data files and app-specific secure-storage entries using your operating system’s tools. Uninstalling alone may leave data or backups. Contact the project for platform-specific help, and remove any backups you control if you also want those copies deleted.
  • Deleting local data does not delete reports you already sent from Gmail or copies held by recipients. Manage those copies in Gmail and with the recipients as appropriate.

5. Security

Google communication uses HTTPS. OAuth authorization uses a system browser, a local loopback callback, PKCE, and a random state value. Account tokens use platform secure storage. These measures do not guarantee complete security. Keep your device, operating system, and app up to date, protect access to your computer, and do not share your tokens.

6. This website

This is a static informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not set app analytics cookies, use advertising trackers, provide an account login, or collect report content. GitHub may process connection and technical information to serve and secure the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to Google or GitHub take you to services governed by their own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. If app functionality changes how personal or Google data is handled, the policy and relevant in-app disclosures will be updated, and additional consent will be requested where required.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or assistance managing local data, contact the maintainer through the DailyReport project issue tracker. Start with a general description and do not post sensitive information publicly.
