01 / REMEMBER

Reminders that fit your day.

Set your working hours and as many reminders as you need. Defaults are 9 AM–6 PM, with reminders at 2 PM, 5 PM, and 6 PM. A centered reminder lets you open the report, snooze, or dismiss.

02 / PREPARE

Your work, brought together.

Choose local Git repositories and collect your commits for today, oldest first. Projects without commits are omitted. Edit the report and add tomorrow’s plans and blockers. Drafts stay on your computer.

03 / SHARE

Review it. Then send it.

Copy a formatted report into your reporting tool, or connect Gmail and reply in your chosen conversation. Preview the report and recipients before clicking Send reply.

Optional Gmail connection

Your account.
Your reporting thread.

Keep daily updates in the conversation your team already uses. DailyReport supports Reply and Reply all, with editable To and CC fields and your existing conversation subject.

The app requests Gmail send permission and message metadata to locate conversations and construct replies. It does not request full email bodies or attachments.

Read the connection steps ↗
  1. Connect your Google account

    Import your Google Desktop OAuth client JSON and sign in through your browser. Account tokens are held in secure storage on your computer.

  2. Choose your report conversation

    Select the thread you normally reply to. You can change it later and set Reply all as the default.

  3. Preview and send

    Review the recipients and formatted report. Sending happens only after you click Send reply. Gmail confirmation completes the report and stops today’s reminders.

Your Google data is used for this reporting workflow. DailyReport does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its use of Google API data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the Google data disclosure.
